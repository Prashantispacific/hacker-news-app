Platform & Customization Guide

An architectural and operational handbook for Hacker News by PG — engineered with extreme breathing room, OLED true black comfort, discussion signal tools, and zero server-side infrastructure.

How the Platform Works

Hacker News by PG operates on a 100% serverless, client-first architecture. There is no custom backend server collecting telemetry or storing sessions. All state resides on your device.

Firebase Real-Time API

Connects to official Hacker News endpoints for instant top, new, best, and deeply nested comment hierarchies.

Algolia HN Index

Powers instant full-text search, historic past editions, and multi-keyword custom channel queries in under 50ms.

IndexedDB + PWA Storage

Custom channels, bookmarked stories, and negative mute rules persist on-device via IndexedDB with offline caching.

Direct URL Fidelity

Links directly to original publisher sites without fragile proxy scrapers, preserving original formatting.

Creating Custom Channel Filters

You can create personalized channels that continuously pull stories from across the entire Hacker News universe based on your exact interests.

Click the [+] New button in the channel bar or the settings modal to open the filter builder.

Concrete Examples

Example 1: Artificial Intelligence & LLM Feed Topic Filter

Filter stories specifically discussing artificial intelligence, foundation models, and transformers:

  • Channel Name: #ai
  • Keywords: ai, llm, gpt, claude, transformer, machine learning
  • Domains: arxiv.org, huggingface.co (optional)
  • Min Score: 50
Example 2: Systems Programming & Rust Language / OS

Filter stories about memory safety, compilers, and low-level engineering:

  • Channel Name: #rust
  • Keywords: rust, cargo, crates.io, memory safety, llvm
  • Domains: github.com, rust-lang.org
  • Min Score: 0 (show all)
Example 3: High-Signal Top Gems Score Filter

Only see generational, viral stories that achieve massive community traction:

  • Channel Name: #gems
  • Keywords: (leave blank to search all topics)
  • Domains: (leave blank)
  • Min Score: 400

💡 Automatic Keyword Inference

If you create a channel named #security, #apple, or #python and leave the Keywords field blank, the platform automatically infers the tag name as the search keyword. You don't need to re-type it manually!

Discussion Signal Suite

Hacker News discussions are where the real value lies. We designed custom ergonomic tools to navigate deep comment trees without losing your place:

Chromatic 2px Guide Rails

Vertical indent lines tinted by depth (white, sky blue, emerald, purple). Clicking any rail folds the entire branch into an inline count.

OP Stepper

The floating ▲ 1 of X ▼ widget jumps straight to replies written by the original poster with smooth scrolling and highlight.

Thread Link Drawer

Clicking "Links" opens a drawer listing every external hyperlink referenced across the entire comment thread with context snippets.

Fold-to-Root

Single toggle in the discussion bar that collapses all nested replies, allowing you to review only top-level root perspectives.

Negative Filtering & Mute Lists

Tired of recurring topics, paywalls, or noisy authors? The platform includes a 100% on-device negative filter.

  1. Click the Settings icon in the top right.
  2. Under Negative Filtering, add any keywords (e.g. crypto, election), domains (e.g. paywall.com), or author usernames.
  3. Matching stories will be filtered immediately before rendering, and a discreet banner will inform you how many stories were muted.
